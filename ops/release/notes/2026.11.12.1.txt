Highlights in novels: select text and press HIGHLIGHT or NOTE in the bar over it (H and N on a keyboard). Three colours, a note on each, DELETE with UNDO. A highlight made offline waits and is sent at the next connection, in novels and in the image reader.
BROWSE: SELECT in a source's catalogue ticks many series, then ADD TO LIBRARY, ADD + FIRST 10 EACH or ADD + ALL adds them in one go, with one UNDO. EXTENSIONS can select many and change their settings at once. Coming back from a series keeps your place in the catalogue.
Sources that need a browser: some extensions only work with a hidden browser. Turn on SETTINGS > BROWSE > WEBVIEW to use them. The first time, it downloads Chromium (about 180 MB). It runs those sites' scripts on your computer in an older browser without a sandbox, so leave it off unless you want such a source. On Linux it needs xvfb (apt install xvfb).
Reading: a double page keeps its own zoom, so your zoom for single pages stays put. Up and down scroll a zoomed page. In novels, SCROLL no longer jumps to the top when it crosses into the next chapter.
The player: >> and << jump a minute, the arrows still 10 seconds. Holding an arrow to seek no longer drops the quality.
The series screen: TAGS and ABOUT sit at the top. A volume's head shows its cover. GROUP AS ONE SHOW merges an anime's seasons into one series, with UNDO and UNGROUP. A one-off kept in a group keeps READ ONLINE and DOWNLOAD.
The library: the CONTINUE row opens the series, ▶ goes straight to the reader, and a long press shows its options. Endless scrolling no longer stalls at the bottom. You can act on a selection made in SEARCH without going back.
TV: BACK asks a second time before leaving the app. The focus outline shows on BROWSE's catalogue, and the series screen's details and chapters are reached as two sections.
