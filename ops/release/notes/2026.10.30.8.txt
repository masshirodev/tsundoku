If something breaks: SETTINGS > LIBRARY > REPORT > SAVE, and send the file. It never carries anything you've hidden, your password or tokens.
FULL SCREEN in the reader's controls hides the browser's bars (Android and computers).
On a phone, the first manga you open says how to turn pages: swipe, or tap the edges.
On a PC, the arrow keys glide a screen at a time, and Ctrl + up/down nudge a little.
A page that didn't load says so at its top, not halfway down a long manhwa page.
